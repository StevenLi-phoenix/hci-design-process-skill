PASS only if ALL hold:
1. Exactly one persona is designated the primary/main persona and at least two others are secondary.
2. Each persona has a name plus goals/motivations and frustrations/pain points that are traceable to the notes.
3. The personas avoid irrelevant details (hobbies or traits unrelated to the problem) and avoid stereotypes.
4. The primary persona combines patterns from more than one participant (a composite archetype, not a renamed copy of one interviewee).
5. Any persona that rests on a single participant is explicitly acknowledged as such, rather than presented as a general archetype.
